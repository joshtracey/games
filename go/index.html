Notes

This is a practical pass-and-play board, not a certified referee. It enforces captures, suicide prevention, and simple ko. Scoring is an estimate using area scoring: stones plus fully surrounded empty territory, with komi added to White. After both players pass, tap a group to mark it dead (tap again to revive it) — dead stones are removed for scoring and their points count as territory.

Exit game?
Your progress will be lost.
